Timeline: A new client moves from first contact to first completed request in approximately one week. This checklist ensures a smooth, consistent onboarding experience.

8-Step Onboarding Process

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Client reaches out. Reply within 1 business day and book a free consultation to discuss their needs.

  2. 2

    Consultation

    Meet with the client. Learn what they need done, how often, and what tools they use. Recommend an appropriate plan.

  3. 3

    Proposal

    Send the plan, pricing, and start date. Note: Prices are shared in sales conversations only, never on the website.

  4. 4

    Agreement

    Send the Service Agreement for signature. Collect first month's payment (ACH or Square).

  5. 5

    Information Sheet

    Send the Client Information Sheet. Request completion within 3 business days to gather account details.

  6. 6

    Set Up

    Create client folder from template, add client to Admin OS, and file signed agreement in folder 01.

  7. 7

    Kickoff

    Send welcome email with request submission process, turnaround times, and direct contact information.

  8. 8

    First Request

    Complete their first request promptly. Follow up after 2 weeks to check progress and address any concerns.
